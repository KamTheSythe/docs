## Overview

The purpose of this proposal is to add a more locally sustainable source of alcohol for bartenders, as well as creating more gameplay and interaction opportunities between botanists and bartenders. 
Though at this moment the purpose of alcohol is merely confined to roleplay (except for a few exceptions), it is possible that more alcoholic mixed drinks could be added to expand on the gameplay of bartenders.

## Background

At the current point in time, the only way of restocking Booze-O-Mat's is through Booze-O-Mat restock boxes ordered through cargo. Bartenders have a tendency to have a lot of down-time in between serving customers,
which is usually time spent idling. Having a method of creating more alcohol could also create more gameplay opportunities for people that aren't assigned the role of bartenders (ie. passanger players setting up a bar in
various maintenance tunnels, as "maints-bars" are often part of maps).

The people wanting to do bartending and mixology could also be influenced to use this mechanic by limiting the amount of available alcohol in Booze-O-Mats, or by creating types of alcohols with unique effects (something akin
to The Doctor's Delight).

## Alcohol Production

To simplify alcohol production, a 2-step process could be used. This would include fermentation and distillation.

### Yeast, Fermentation Vessel and the Distiller

To start alcohol production, one would have to acquire yeast. As yeast is a type of fungus, it could be acquired from botany (crafted using Chantarelle/Linghzi mushrooms), or found in maintenance tunnels (crafted using Spaceshrooms).
The next step would be creating a vessel to brew alcohol. In real life, large metal vats with plumbing are used to do so.
- Fermentation Vessel (10 steel, 2 LV coil, Construction Menu - A large free-standing metal vessel, featuring built-in plumbing for "burping" alcohol, as well as a heating apparatus.)
Fermentation alone could be used to create weaker alcoholic drinks (beers, lagers, wines) or creating malt as precursor (whisky malt, vodka malt), however, to acquire more potent liqour, the next step would be distillation.
- Distiller (5 steel, 2 LV coil, Construction Menu - A distiller for... Distilling alcohol. A large metal keg designed to be heated to boil off excess water from weaker alcohols, to make them more potent.)
A distiller would be used to create more potent alcohols from their corresponding versions of malts (berry wine -> gin, whisky malt -> whisky, vodka malt -> vodka) , or distillation of pure ethanol from strong liqours (vodka -> ethanol).
